## Scope

[Back to navigation](#navigation)

Id families: `NFR-*`, `D-*`, `R-*`, `A-*`, `Q-*` and `FR-*`. Each is an extraction id: outside the repo; not defined in the SRS.

The SRS defines only `REQ-*` and `AC-*`. The PROPOSAL cites the ids below for traceability to those outside files.

No definition, title or meaning here. The origin files are not in this repo. Not verified.

Line numbers refer to [PROPOSAL.md](../roadmap/PROPOSAL.md). Short forms such as `R1` or `A3` also appear there. They are listed as written.
